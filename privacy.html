AEGIS PRIME Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AEGIS PRIME ("the Bot") processes information when installed in or used with a Discord server.

1. Information AEGIS PRIME May Process

AEGIS PRIME processes information necessary to provide its moderation, administration, alliance, event, support, backup, and automation features. Depending on how a server is configured, this may include:

  • Discord server, user, role, channel, and message identifiers.
  • Server configuration and feature settings.
  • Alliance membership, rank, and approval information.
  • Nickname and in-game-name verification information.
  • Warnings, moderation actions, staff notes, and related administrative records.
  • Support tickets, reports, and associated administrative information.
  • Poll, announcement, event, and scheduling information.
  • User notification preferences.
  • Backup and recovery information associated with configured Discord servers.
  • Developer feedback submitted through AEGIS PRIME.
  • Operational logs required to maintain security, reliability, and bot functionality.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by AEGIS PRIME is used to:

  • Operate server-management and moderation features.
  • Manage roles, channels, alliances, permissions, and member workflows.
  • Provide tickets, reports, announcements, polls, events, and scheduled actions.
  • Detect configuration drift and restore managed server components.
  • Create backups and support recovery operations.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve the Bot.
  • Receive and respond to bug reports, suggestions, and developer feedback.

3. Information Sharing

AEGIS PRIME does not sell personal information. Information is not provided to advertisers for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed through Discord and the infrastructure used to host AEGIS PRIME as necessary to provide the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, communities, the Bot, or its infrastructure.

4. Data Storage

AEGIS PRIME stores server-specific configuration and operational data required for its enabled features. Production data and backups are maintained on the infrastructure used to operate the Bot.

Each Discord server is logically separated by its server identifier so that one server's configuration is not intentionally exposed to another server.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AEGIS PRIME's configured features, maintain backups, support moderation history, or satisfy security and operational requirements.

Some records may remain temporarily in backups after deletion from active data until those backups are rotated or replaced.

6. User and Server Data Requests

Where supported, users may use AEGIS PRIME's data and privacy commands, including the available /aegis mydata functionality, to review information associated with their use of the Bot.

Server owners may remove AEGIS PRIME from their Discord server at any time. Questions or requests concerning stored data may also be submitted through the Bot's Developer Feedback system.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to protect AEGIS PRIME and its stored data. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Discord

AEGIS PRIME operates through Discord and is also subject to Discord's platform, privacy, and developer policies. Discord independently processes information according to its own privacy practices.

9. Children's Privacy

AEGIS PRIME is intended for use in accordance with Discord's age requirements. Server owners and administrators are responsible for ensuring their communities comply with Discord's applicable rules and age requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AEGIS PRIME evolves. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, bug reports, or other concerns may be submitted through AEGIS PRIME's built-in Developer Feedback system or by contacting the developer on Discord: shadowwalker3114.
